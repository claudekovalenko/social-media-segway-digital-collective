Who this is for

Digital Collective provides creators with a link and a page, collects responses from people who visit that page, and follows up with them. By registering as a creator or submitting a response, you agree to these terms.

Creators

Creators keep ownership of their own content and their own audience. Creators agree to the Statement of Faith and the creator commitments, to use only content they have the right to use, and to keep their link name free of impersonation or reserved words. The collective may suspend a creator page that breaks these terms, keeping past records intact.

Responses and follow-up

When you submit a form, you are asking the collective and the creator whose link you used to contact you about your next step. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying to any message or writing to hello@digitalcollective.com. How we handle your information is described in the Privacy & Consent page.

Content is the creator’s own

Creator content is not vetted or endorsed by the collective. Listing a creator is not an endorsement of everything they teach. Test everything against Scripture and take what you learn to a local pastor.

No cost

The collective is free for creators. There are no payments, subscriptions, or fees in this version.
