What we believe

Statement of faith

Draft — review this wording with your leaders before launch. It follows historic creedal Christianity; adjust emphasis to your community's convictions.

God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — the Creator and Sustainer of all things.

The Scriptures

We believe the Bible — the sixty-six books of the Old and New Testaments — is the inspired Word of God, breathed out by Him through human authors. It is inerrant in all that it affirms and infallible in all that it teaches: it does not fail, and it does not mislead.

Scripture is our final and highest authority for faith, doctrine, and life — above tradition, experience, feeling, and cultural opinion. Nothing is to be added to it or taken from it. Where God has spoken, the matter is settled.

We uphold this together. Every creator in Digital Collective commits to teaching in agreement with Scripture, handling it carefully and in context, and submitting their content — and their own lives — to its authority. Discipleship in this collective means forming people by the Word of God, not by opinion or trend.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, born of a virgin, that He lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, rose bodily from the dead, ascended to the Father, and will return in glory.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone — not by works — and that everyone who turns from sin and trusts in Jesus is made new and adopted into God's family.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit indwells every believer, empowering us to live like Christ, to make disciples, and to serve one another with the gifts He gives.

The Church

We believe the Church is the body of Christ — every believer, everywhere — called to gather regularly, to love one another, to celebrate baptism and communion, and to multiply healthy communities of disciples in every place.

What we endorse — and what we don't

Digital Collective is a hub, not a broadcaster. Every creator here signs our statement of faith and is asked to hold to it. Their content is not vetted by us, and appearing in this collective is not our endorsement of any particular teaching, opinion, book, ministry, or practice a creator puts out. Each creator is responsible before God for what they teach.

Our purpose is narrower and simpler than approval: to help people take a first step toward Christ and then into healthy, in-person community. What you find here — a video, a course, an online group — is a preliminary entry point, not a substitute for a local church.

So be a Berean. The believers in Berea “were of more noble character than those in Thessalonica, for they received the word with all eagerness, examining the Scriptures daily to see if these things were so” (Acts 17:11). Test everything you hear here — from any creator, and from us — against the Word of God. Hold on to what is good.

We warmly encourage you to bring what you're learning to a trusted local pastor or mature believer who knows you, can answer your questions in person, and can walk with you over time. That relationship is the goal; this platform only points toward it.

Disciple-making

We believe every follower of Jesus is called to make disciples: to help people know God, grow in His image, and gather in healthy, reproducing communities — whether in an established local church or a simple gathering that multiplies.
