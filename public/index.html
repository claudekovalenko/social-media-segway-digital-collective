One link. Three steps.

Every creator gets a personal link for their bio. When someone taps it, they walk a simple path:

  1. Know Goda clear gospel invitation and a first response
  2. Grow with Godonline discipleship in weekly small groups
  3. Gather Locallyjoining a healthy church — or starting a gathering that multiplies

Every response is tracked to your link, and you can see the people God is bringing through your work.
