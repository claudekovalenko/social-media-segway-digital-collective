What creators commit to

  • Teach in agreement with our Statement of Faith.
  • Point people toward a local church, not only toward yourself.
  • Handle every response with care: real people, real consent.
  • Keep your own voice. The collective follows up; it never edits you.

Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. Free for creators.

Who owns the responses?

You see everyone who came through your link. The collective follows up and reports the totals.

Can I use my own videos?

Yes. The three steps are fixed; what's inside them is yours.

Do I stay independent?

Yes. Your platform, your voice. Creators sign our Statement of Faith; content isn't vetted or endorsed by us.
