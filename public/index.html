What creators commit to

  • Teach in agreement with our Statement of Faith, including the inerrancy and final authority of Scripture.
  • Point people toward a local church, not only toward yourself.
  • Handle every response with care: real people, real consent, real follow-up.
  • Keep your own voice. The network never edits your content; it only follows up.

We're a network, not a broadcaster. Creators sign the statement, but their content isn't vetted or endorsed by us. Test everything against Scripture (Acts 17:11).

Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. The network is free for creators at launch, with no payment or subscription features.

Who owns the responses?

You see every response that came through your link and can export them any time. The network sees the same records, aggregated across every creator, so it can follow up and report.

Can I use my own videos?

Yes. Each of the three sections holds your content: video, text, images, files, and links. The three steps are fixed; what's inside them is yours.

Who follows up?

The network does, in your name, with an approved message you can personalize. A reported commitment is routed straight to your Grow with God section.

How does church connection work?

Get Connected sends people to a healthy church near them through our partner VisitorReach, and the click is tracked back to you.

Do I stay independent?

Yes. Your platform, your audience, your voice. The network provides the link, the data, and the follow-up.
