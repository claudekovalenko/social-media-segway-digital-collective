What people will read alongside your link

So the whole weight does not rest on one link, every Get Connected page carries the same short guide to choosing a church well.

  • Be a Berean. Check what any church teaches against Scripture (Acts 17:11). A healthy church welcomes that.
  • Look for the Bible preached, Jesus at the centre, the gospel clear, leaders who are accountable, and people who know each other.
  • Warning signs: one leader nobody may question, money pressure, secrets, cutting you off from family and friends, or a teaching that only they have the truth.
  • Not a cult. You can visit, ask questions and leave. The door is open both ways.
