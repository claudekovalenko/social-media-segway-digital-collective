Privacy & consent

Draft — this is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice. Have a licensed attorney review it before launch, especially if you will contact people in the EU/UK (GDPR), California (CCPA), or send SMS (TCPA).

What we collect

When you fill out a form on this site we collect what you type: your name, email, and — if you choose to share them — phone number, city, country, preferred language, your responses, and the creator whose link brought you here.

Your consent to be contacted

By checking the consent box and submitting a form, you give Digital Collective and the creator whose link you used permission to contact you at the email (and phone number, if provided) you shared — about the step you took, the group or gathering you asked about, and closely related follow-up. We record the date and time of your consent.

What we don't do

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with anyone beyond Digital Collective, the creator whose link you used, and the group leaders needed to connect you. We won't add you to unrelated mailing lists.

Where your data lives

Your information is stored in a secure database and is accessible only to Digital Collective administrators and, for your own responses, the creator whose link you used.

Opting out

You can withdraw consent at any time. Reply to any message we send with "stop", or contact us and ask for your information to be corrected or deleted. We will honor deletion requests promptly.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
